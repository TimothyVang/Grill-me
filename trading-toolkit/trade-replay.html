2 · The method (same on every market — win or lose)
  1. Mark your levelSupport/resistance, prior-day high/low, or the all-time high — the price the market has reacted to before.
  2. Confirm the trendOn the 5-min: 9-EMA above the 21, price above VWAP. Trade with that direction.
  3. Draw Fibonacci & wait for the pocketSwing low → high. Let price pull back into the 0.618 golden pocket, ideally on your level. That overlap is confluence.
  4. Wait for the candleA hammer or engulfing candle must print in the pocket and close. That's your trigger — never enter before it closes.
  5. Enter with defined riskStop just beyond the pocket (below 0.786). Target the prior high. Size so the stop = ≤1% of your account.
  6. Manage — and accept the outcomeDon't touch the stop. If it wins, take the target. If it loses, take the small loss and move on. Log both.
Why the losing replay matters more Flip to ✕ Stopped out and watch: the setup looks identical until price rolls over and breaks the pocket. You can't tell winners from losers in advance — nobody can. What you can control is that the loss is −1R and boring, not an account-ender. Notice the loss (~$15–$30 on a micro) is a fraction of the +5R win. Small losses, bigger wins, over many trades — that's the entire game.
Honest caption These are two clean illustrations, not a promise. Real charts are messier and real win rates are often 40–55%. No method predicts which trades win. The stop and the 1% rule are what let you be wrong repeatedly and still survive to catch the winners. The journal is where you learn your real numbers.
