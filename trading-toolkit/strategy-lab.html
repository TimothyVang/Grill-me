Read this before you trust a number A great backtest is not a promise. Beware overfitting — if you tweak rules until the sample looks perfect, you've curve-fit to noise. The samples here are randomly generated, so a strategy that "wins" on one may lose on the next “New sample data” — that variance is the honest lesson. Test on real, out-of-sample candles, expect a lower live win rate, and never risk money on a rule set you haven't also forward-tested on a paper account.

Education, not financial advice. A backtester measures how rules would have behaved on the data you give it — it does not predict the future, and past performance never guarantees future results. Companion to the Confluence Playbook & Trade Journal.
