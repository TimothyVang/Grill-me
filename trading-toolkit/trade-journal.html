How to read this Expectancy is the number that matters most: your average return per trade in R (multiples of the risk you planned). Positive expectancy = a real edge. Profit factor above ~1.3 is healthy. One or two trades tell you nothing — judge the numbers only after 30+ trades, and the same goes per market below: don't judge Bitcoin on 4 trades.
