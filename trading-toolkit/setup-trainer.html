How you're scored You're graded on the decision, not the outcome — because that's how real trading works. Taking a valid setup is a right call even when it loses (good setups still lose ~40% of the time). Passing a trap is a right call even though you "missed" nothing. Read the candle at the level: a rejection/hammer = trigger; a weak candle closing at its lows = no trade. Reset clears your record; your stats save in this browser.

Education, not financial advice. These are generated practice setups to train pattern recognition — they don't predict real markets. Your real edge only shows in a live journal over many trades. Companion to The Confluence Playbook, Trade Replay, and Trade Journal.
