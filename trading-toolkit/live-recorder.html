Must be opened as a local file This page fetches a live feed, so it only works when you open the file directly in your browser (double-click live-recorder.html) or serve it locally — not from a sandboxed hosted artifact, which blocks outside connections. If the status shows a network error, your connection may be down or the exchange may be geo-restricting; try a different market.
Why crypto only Bitcoin/ETH/SOL have free, keyless, real-time feeds. Oil and S&P futures data requires a paid CME subscription, so it isn't recordable for free here — practice the method on crypto candles, which trade 24/7 and behave like any other market for charting purposes. Recorded candles are saved in your browser and survive a refresh.
